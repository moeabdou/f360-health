How the Horizon works

Data becomes direction.

The Horizon is not a test; it is a practice. Every Membership begins with the first read of your Blueprint, and from there the work is ongoing — read, refined, and re-pointed as your life moves.

01

Your Horizon

The reference point

The specific life you've named — drawn from your 2040 Horizon reflection, held as the line every result is measured against. The difference between data and direction.

02

Your Blueprint

What's true about you

Your biological baseline begins with a blood panel, and widens as your Horizon calls for it — your genome and phenome, the microbiome, cognitive and cardiometabolic measures, advanced imaging and body-composition scans, and your own notes — held in one place and read as a whole. The first draw is included with your Membership.

03

Your Pathway

What we do about it

A short, deliberate set of actionable possibilities — set with the Forward 360 clinical team and co-designed with you, in a private session, in plain language. You remain the author of every decision.

04

The Vitality Loop

The cadence

Every four months, a fresh reading re-points the focus. A single test is a number; a rhythm of them is a trajectory — measured, and yours. This is how the Horizon evolves with you.

Designed with the Forward 360 clinical team, and always in coordination with your own physician — never in place of them.

What we measure

It begins with blood. It does not end there.

Designed by the Forward 360 clinical team and reviewed with you line by line, against your Horizon.

The first read — your blood panel

Metabolic

Fasting glucose, fasting insulin, HbA1c — how your body handles fuel today, and where it's headed.

Cardiovascular

The full lipid picture, including ApoB and Lp(a) — the markers that predict.

Inflammation

hs-CRP, homocysteine, and a complete blood count — the quiet signals that precede the loud ones.

Hormonal

Thyroid, cortisol, and the sex hormones — the systems that set your energy, recovery, and drive.

Organ function

Liver and kidney markers, including cystatin-C — the infrastructure the rest depends on.

Micronutrients

Vitamin D, B12, ferritin, magnesium — the inputs that are simplest to correct and most often overlooked.

The fuller picture — as your Horizon calls for it

Genome & phenome

The fixed code and the living readout — genetic possibility turned into clinical foresight.

The microbiome

The gut as a system of systems — a working lever in metabolism, immunity, and mood.

Cognitive & brain health

Baselines for memory, processing, and long-term neurological resilience.

Advanced imaging & body composition

Full-body scans and composition measures — structure, not just chemistry.

The exact program is set by the clinical team, sequenced to your Horizon, and refined as the field moves — always in coordination with your own physician.

For Nurse Practitioners

Renaissance

For a century, medicine has been organized around illness. The next era belongs to wellbeing — measured, personal, designed — and no clinician is better positioned to deliver it than the nurse practitioner. Renaissance is a six-week salon: one question each week, worked at one table, led by the scientist who made wellbeing measurable and the Yale practitioner building it into daily practice — for the NPs who intend to lead the era, not adjust to it.

One question each week. The whole table works it. Not a course you complete — the first tier of a collective you belong to.
