For Nurse Practitioners

Renaissance

For a century, medicine has been organized around illness. The next era belongs to wellbeing — measured, personal, designed — and no clinician is better positioned to deliver it than the nurse practitioner. Renaissance is a six-week salon: one question each week, worked at one table, led by the scientist who made wellbeing measurable and the Yale practitioner building it into daily practice — for the NPs who intend to lead the era, not adjust to it.

One question each week. The whole table works it. Not a course you complete — the first tier of a collective you belong to.
